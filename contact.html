Quick answers

Do you take sample or small orders?+

Yes – send the details in an enquiry and we'll confirm samples and minimum quantities for your product.

Can you make my own design or label?+

Yes. Share a sketch, photo or reference and we'll suggest fabric, print and finishing for private-label production.

How quickly will I get a reply?+

Enquiries arrive on WhatsApp, so we usually respond during working hours.
