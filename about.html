About us

Every cloth is a journey.

Vastrayan comes from vastra (cloth) and ayan (journey) – the path a thread takes to become fabric and then clothing. We carry that idea into everything we make: patient craft, honest material and modern machines working together.

We are a Maharashtra-based textile unit that makes fabrics, ethnic wear, casual wear, knitwear, kidswear and yarn – and takes private-label work from first sketch to packed cartons. One team handles every stage, so what you approve as a sample is what arrives in bulk.

Our story

From a single loom to a full textile unit

We began with a simple belief: good clothing starts with good cloth. Today our work spans yarn, weaving, dyeing and printing, cutting, stitching and packing – so every stage is planned by people who understand the next one.

Whether you are a retailer stocking ethnic wear, a boutique after linen casuals or a brand launching a private label, we work to your brief and keep you updated from sample to dispatch.
